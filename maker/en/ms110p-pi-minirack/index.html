3D-printed mini rack for MS110P and Raspberry Pi

A mini rack that stands Raspberry Pi boards upright on top of a MERCUSYS MS110P PoE switch. Pi 3B+ and Zero 2 W boards slide into five rails, and an L-shaped cover closes the roof and the back.

How it fits together

  • Bottom The MS110P sits in the base cradle (P01), and the side frames (P02) form the pillars.
  • Middle Carriers slide into the rails of the deck (P03). The Pi 3B+ carrier P04 takes one rail, and the Zero 2 W carrier P08 takes two.
  • Top The L-shaped cover (P09) closes the roof and the back of the Pi layer. The front and sides stay open.

Printing and assembly

  • Made for a Bambu Lab P1S with a 0.4 mm nozzle. The build in the photo has its teal parts printed in PETG and its black parts in PLA. The STL files are saved in their print orientation, so import them at 100% as they are.
  • Print one carrier first and check that it slides into a rail before printing the rest.
  • Assemble in this order: base and frames → switch and deck → carriers with their boards → cables and cover. To swap an SD card, undo the two bolts at the back of the cover and slide the carrier out to the front.

What has been checked
