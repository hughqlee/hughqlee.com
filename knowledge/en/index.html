  3. 2026-10-23 · Robotics and automation

    Hydraulic and Pneumatic Microinjectors: Set the Pressure, or Set the Volume?

    A microinjector pushes the sample from behind the needle with air (pneumatic) or with oil (hydraulic). Air compresses easily and oil hardly at all, so a pneumatic injector sets the pressure and the time, and a hydraulic one sets the volume. Change the needle tip and the sample, and see how much each delivers and what pressure it takes. The second page on robotics and automation.
