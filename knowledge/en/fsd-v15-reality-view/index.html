Uncompromising engineering: FSD v15 Reality View

On 2026-11-21, FSD v15 replaced the in-car screen with Reality View. The scene looks filmed from the real road, but it is not camera video: the driving AI draws it. Ashok Elluswamy, who leads Tesla AI, said the view is not only there to look good but shows what the car intends, where it will turn and where it will stop. How did Tesla draw a new view without taking compute away from driving?

It keeps the driving path as it is and sets a 1B Diffusion head beside it on the spare compute (half of the three TRIPs on SoC B), so it gains a new view without taking anything from driving.

Turn off the view

Turn off the view in three driving AIs. Set beside the other two models, Tesla's structure is plain to see.

Three TRIPs on SoC B · half of one for the view

Driving (the 3B model) and the view (the 1B Diffusion model) share one UVE output.

The action reads ② (the K/V cache); the view reads ① and ③.

The action reads ②, which holds the reasoning text too.

Sources: two replies by Musk on X (2026-11-22), the Qwen-Drive-1.0 paper §2.1, the Alpamayo-R1 paper §3.2.2 and §6.2 Table 8

How it works

  1. Look once, use it twiceTesla reads the camera video once with its vision encoder (UVE) and makes a Hidden Feature: a bundle of numbers inside the model that stands for the images. The 3B model that drives and the 1B Diffusion model that draws the view both read it.
  2. The view is a branch beside drivingThe view does not pass through the 3B model, so turning it off leaves what driving reads unchanged. The other two models show the difference. In Qwen-Drive the view (a BEV perception head, a map seen from above) is also a branch beside driving, but it reads separate values from before the language model and after it. In Alpamayo the view (the text that gives the reasons for its decision) sits in front of driving, so removing the text changes how it drives. Across 75 hard scenarios in a simulator, a model trained to output only a trajectory, with no text, came too close to another road user in 17% of them. The model that writes the text did so in 11%.
  3. It draws with the spare computeThe view gets the compute that is left over. According to Musk, after a small decoder moved into the chip's fast memory (SRAM), only half of one of the three compute engines (TRIPs) on the second chip (SoC B) was left. The 1B Diffusion model draws with that half. It does not start from scratch each time: it adds noise to the previous view, redraws it as the current one, and puts out 8 frames at a time (36 fps).

A new view was gained, and driving's share was left alone. In the same v15, Tesla said its handling of safety-critical cases got 25 times better, but it did not credit that to the design of the view.

Common misconceptions

MisconceptionReality View spreads the camera video out by depth and paints it with color.

In factA 1B diffusion model draws it. The video maker who imitated that depth approach on a MacBook also said it differs from Tesla's.

MisconceptionTesla made the view lighter and moved the saved compute to driving.

In factMusk describes the reverse order: the half that was left went to the view. He did not say whether it uses less compute than the old view.

Frequently asked questions

What is Tesla FSD v15 Reality View?

It is the new in-car screen of FSD v15, which began rolling out on 2026-11-21. It does not show the camera video as it is: a diffusion model of about 1B parameters takes the visual features of the driving model and draws a realistic scene. Arrows for the turns ahead and pins for the parking spot also show what the car intends to do.

What model draws Reality View?

According to Musk, a conditional mean-flow (diffusion) model of about 1B parameters. It branches off the same UVE features that feed the 3B model producing the driving actions, adds noise to the previous view and redraws it as the current one, and puts out 8 frames at a time (36 fps).

Does Reality View take compute away from FSD driving?

According to Musk, after a small decoder moved into SRAM only half of one of the three TRIPs on SoC B was left, and Reality View is drawn with that remainder. The branch that draws the view does not feed into the 3B model that produces the driving actions.

How do Qwen-Drive and Alpamayo make their view?

In Qwen-Drive-1.0 a BEV perception head reads the features before and after the language model and draws 3D boxes, occupancy, and a map, while a Planning Expert that reads the language model's K/V cache produces the driving plan. Alpamayo writes its reasoning as text instead of a 3D view, and its Action Expert reads that text too before producing a trajectory.
