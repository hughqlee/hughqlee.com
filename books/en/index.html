How to borrow

  1. Pick a book. Choose one on the shelf and tell me a week before the meetup; I bring it.
  2. Leave the deposit. Send the deposit shown on the book's card to the deposit-only account.
  3. Take it. At the meetup we photograph the book's condition together. If you cannot come, I send it by parcel, cash on delivery.
  4. Read it. You have four weeks, until the next meetup. If nobody has reserved the book, you can keep it once more, until the meetup after that.
  5. Return it. Bring it to the next meetup: I check its condition there and refund the whole deposit. If you rate it, your stars and review appear on the book's card (optional).

To return a book by parcel, prepay the shipping. You can borrow up to two books at a time.

The deposit and settlement

The deposit is what the same book costs new at an online bookstore today: usually 90% of the list price, rounded down to the nearest 1,000 won.

If the book is damaged or does not come back, the deposit settles it and the book becomes the borrower's. The borrower has in effect bought a new copy, and I have the money to buy one again. Because the deposit is exactly the price of a new copy, nobody gains from a settlement.

Condition criteria

Fine: full refundDamaged: settled, the book is the borrower's
Signs of opening, creases on the spineWater, drink, or food stains; pages wavy from getting wet
Everyday wear on the corners and coverTears, missing pages, a loose cover
One or two lightly folded cornersNotes or underlining in pen or highlighter
Anything else that is unclearLost, or neither the book nor word from the borrower two weeks after the due date

I compare the book with the photos we took when it was lent, and when in doubt it counts as fine. Check the book yourself before returning it; if it is damaged, just tell me instead of sending it.

Stars and reviews

Ratings are out of three stars.

  • ★★★ 3 stars: Recommended
  • ★★★ 2 stars: Pretty good, not quite satisfying
  • ★★★ 1 star: Not really recommended

I rate each of my books too, and write a review only when I have something to say.

Readers who borrowed a book rate it when they return it, so every reader rating here comes from someone who actually borrowed and read this copy. A rating has up to four parts:

  • A nickname: no real names or photos.
  • Stars: one to three.
  • One line (optional): up to 100 characters, with no endings or twists.
  • Who it is for (optional): up to 30 characters.

One star is a useful review. A book that gets both three stars and one star becomes a candidate for discussion at the book club.

This shelf makes no money

  • No lending fees, late fees, or membership dues.
  • No sponsored or complimentary copies, and no affiliate links.
  • Deposits sit untouched in a dedicated account, whose balance always equals the deposits for the books on loan.
  • Every book on the shelf was bought by me.

Lending terms

Read the full terms · draft, 2026-10-12

This translation is for reference; the Korean terms govern.

  1. Purpose

    These terms set out how Hugh Q Lee (the "owner") lends books from the owner's shelf free of charge.

  2. Definitions

    A "reader" is a person who borrows a book. The "deposit" is money a reader leaves to secure the book's return. "Settlement" means using the deposit to compensate for a book that is damaged, lost, or not returned.

  3. Free lending

    Lending is free of charge (a loan for use under Article 609 of the Korean Civil Act). The owner takes no money of any kind: no lending fee, late fee, membership due, or commission.

  4. Who can borrow

    Adults among the owner's friends and book-club members. One person can borrow up to two books at a time.

  5. Deposit

    1. The deposit is the amount shown on the shelf: the price of the same book new at an online bookstore, rounded down to the nearest 1,000 won.
    2. The reader sends the deposit to the owner's deposit-only account before receiving the book.
    3. The owner keeps the deposit untouched.
    4. When a reader returns one book and borrows another on the same day, only the difference between the deposits changes hands.

  6. Loan period

    Four weeks, until the next book-club meetup. If nobody has reserved the book, the reader may extend it once, to the meetup after that.

  7. Hand-off and shipping

    1. Books are handed over in person as a rule.
    2. When a book goes by parcel, the owner sends it cash on delivery, and the reader prepays its return.
    3. Whoever sends a parcel bears the risk of its loss or damage in transit.

  8. Return and refund

    The owner refunds the whole deposit on the day the returned book's condition is checked.

  9. Condition check

    The owner compares the book with the photos taken together when it was lent, following the condition criteria on this page. When in doubt, the book counts as fine.

  10. Settlement

    1. If a book is damaged or lost, the deposit is applied as agreed damages (Article 398 of the Civil Act).
    2. If neither the book nor word from the reader arrives within two weeks after the due date (or the extended due date), the book counts as lost and is settled.
    3. On settlement, the rights to the book pass to the reader (Article 399 of the Civil Act).
    4. If damage is found after a book is returned, the owner sends photos and ships the book back cash on delivery; if the reader does not want it, the owner donates it. The owner never keeps both a settled deposit and the book.

  11. Publishing reviews

    1. Ratings and reviews are optional. Only those the reader agrees to publish appear on this page, under a nickname.
    2. The owner may fix typos but does not change the meaning, and does not publish spoilers or attacks on others.
    3. On request, a review is taken down from the page. This site is published from a public repository, so earlier versions may remain in its history.

  12. Personal information

    1. The owner collects only what lending needs: a name, contact details, a refund account, and, for parcels, an address.
    2. It is used only for lending, contact, and refunds, and is not given to anyone else.
    3. Once a refund or settlement is complete, the contact details, address, and account are deleted from the loan ledger, leaving only a nickname.

  13. Changes

    Changes to these terms are announced on this page. A loan already under way follows the terms in force when it began.
