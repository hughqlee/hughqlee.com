How to borrow

  1. Pick a book on the shelf and tell me in an Instagram DM.
  2. Send a deposit equal to the price of a new copy.
  3. Get it in person, or by parcel, cash on delivery.
  4. Return it within four weeks and get the whole deposit back.

The deposit

There are no lending or late fees. You leave a deposit equal to the price of a new copy and get all of it back when you return the book.

If the book is damaged or doesn't come back, the deposit covers it and the book is yours. Signs of reading are fine; only water damage, tears, or writing count as damage.

Stars and reviews

  • ★★★ 3 stars: Recommended
  • ★★★ 2 stars: Pretty good, not quite satisfying
  • ★★★ 1 star: Not really recommended

Readers who borrowed a book can leave stars and a one-line review when they return it.

Lending terms
  1. Lending is free: no lending fee, late fee, or any other charge.
  2. Friends can borrow up to two books at a time.
  3. The deposit is the price of a new copy and is refunded in full on return.
  4. A loan lasts four weeks and can be extended once if nobody has reserved the book.
  5. Books are handed over in person or sent by parcel: cash on delivery on the way out, prepaid on the way back.
  6. If a book is damaged or lost, the deposit settles it and the book becomes the borrower's.
  7. A book with no return and no word two weeks after the due date counts as lost.
  8. Stars and reviews are left only by those who want to, under a nickname.
  9. Contact details and refund accounts are used only for the loan and deleted once it is settled.
